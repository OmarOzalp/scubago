## Notes from execution (2026-09-15)

- **Blocking bug found and fixed:** Metro resolved `three` through its `require` export condition to `build/three.cjs`, a deprecated CommonJS shim whose top-level `process.emitWarning` call is undefined under Hermes, so importing `@react-three/fiber` threw "undefined is not a function" at startup. `metro.config.js` now resolves every `three` import to `build/three.module.js`.
- `simctl io booted screenshot` does not include expo-gl's CAEAGLLayer on this simulator (it did once, under a system alert). The live screen and real devices are unaffected; verification used `GLView.takeSnapshotAsync` from inside the app instead.

- R3F 9.7's native entry patches three's FileLoader through `expo-file-system/legacy`, which SDK 57 still exports; the app nonetheless loads GLBs itself (`Asset.loadAsync` → `File.arrayBuffer` → `GLTFLoader.parse`) so dev, release and web share one path and loading/failure states are explicit rather than Suspense-driven.
- Only shark, ray and fish categories have rigs (81 of 119 catalog species). Other residents remain in the collection list below the scene; when none of a user's residents has a rig the scene shows the labeled preview animals instead of an empty ocean.
- Up to 8 animals swim at once (skinned + shadowed meshes); larger collections rotate pages every 30 s, as the 2D scene did with 12.
